{% blocktrans %}Seurch is a privacy-first metasearch engine. It queries several independent providers at once and blends their results into one unbiased list, so you get broad coverage without handing your search history to a single company.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Web results come from Brave, Mojeek, Marginalia and Staan, while richer answers draw on Wikipedia, Stack Exchange, OpenStreetMap and other open sources. You decide which to use from your settings.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Seurch is built in Europe and is still in alpha, so expect the occasional rough edge as it grows. Thanks for trying it early.{% endblocktrans %}
